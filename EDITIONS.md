## 2026-10-27

The English list's final review, and the first glosses in other languages. **2,553 items, 2,268 default**, 285 hidden (was 2,556, 2,274 and 282).

**What changed since 2026-10-24, in brief:** two items deleted as duplicates (O010 *many*, P095 *(be) ill*) and one (P024b *last*); three snail items made hidden sub-items of H055 *snail*; H, O and P renumbered accordingly; two items redesignated (D040 *aardwolf*, H012 *mud wasp*); four verbs and eight glosses renamed; Canadian spelling; the property items as *(be) big*; regions on five animals; new and revised Clarifications across every section; French, Arabic and Amharic glosses for every item and 119 ready-made ethnic groups. **A database built on 2026-10-24 is brought up to date with `conversions/2026-10-24 to 2026-10-27.tsv`** (old and new reference; `-` for a deleted item), the form Lemmatrix's *Convert references from a table* reads.

- **Glosses in other languages** (`glosses/`): French, Arabic and Amharic for the whole list (sections A to V) and the ethnic groups (`ethnic_groups.tsv`), machine audited only so far.
- **Ethnic groups, ready to insert:** `ethnic_groups.tsv`, 119 groups in the C + ISO 639-3 pattern (CAMH *Amhara person*, CAMHf, CAMHm), 357 items, all hidden and **not counted in the list's totals**, each with its regional code and a Clarification naming the language its code comes from. Clarifications sharpened from the ethnic groups' glosses (Josh, 27 October): CAFR *Afrikaner*, CKIN, CKON, CLIN, CRUN, CSAG, CTIG, CTMH. One code per ethnonym: where one name covers several ISO languages, the macrolanguage code (CARA *Arab*, CSWA *Swahili*, CFUL *Fulani*).

## 2026-10-24

The amendments after the final audit. 2,556 items, **2,274 default**, 282 hidden.

- **Made default:** B044f *female prostitute*; C015f, C015m, C019f, C019m (cousins).
- **Hidden:** B044 *prostitute*; C009 *child (offspring)*; C012–C024, the generic grandparents, piblings, cousins and in-laws, whose female and male forms are the default.
- **Correspondences:** family links for the human terms (sections B and C): an ungendered CAWL or Swadesh item on the generic also links to its female and male forms, and a gendered pair also links to its generic, except in discrete families (B001, B004, C009, C010, C012, C016, C020, C021, C023, C024). 153 links added (149 CAWL, 4 Swadesh).
- Clarifications revised for A035b, A073b and C009; C008f and C008m shortened.
