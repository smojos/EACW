## 2026-10-24

The amendments after the final audit. 2,556 items, **2,274 default**, 282 hidden.

- **Made default:** B044f *female prostitute*; C015f, C015m, C019f, C019m (cousins).
- **Hidden:** B044 *prostitute*; C009 *child (offspring)*; C012–C024, the generic grandparents, piblings, cousins and in-laws, whose female and male forms are the default.
- **Correspondences:** family links for the human terms (sections B and C): an ungendered CAWL or Swadesh item on the generic also links to its female and male forms, and a gendered pair also links to its generic, except in discrete families (B001, B004, C009, C010, C012, C016, C020, C021, C023, C024). 153 links added (149 CAWL, 4 Swadesh).
- Clarifications revised for A035b, A073b and C009; C008f and C008m shortened.
